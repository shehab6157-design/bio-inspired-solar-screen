A screen that lives off the light around it

Moth eyes, firefly lanterns, leaf antennas and hummingbird torpor, tested together as layers of one screen. Move the light and watch what each device can do. This page dims and brightens with it.

Chameleon mode

A chameleon changes its skin to its surroundings. This screen reflects room light like paper when it's bright, and glows like a normal phone screen when it's dim. Compare it with a phone that only glows and a screen that only reflects.

Whose day?

Torpor: getting through dim days

A hummingbird slows its body down at night to live on its reserves. This e-paper device does the same: it refreshes often when its store is full and rests when it runs low. Here are 7 days, 120 simulated devices each.
